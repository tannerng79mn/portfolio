01 — About

A non-linear path
built on one constant:
asking better questions.

Mortgage Underwriting
↓ Restaurant Management
↓ Allianz · Insurance Consulting
↓ Machine Learning Engineering (Building Now)

I didn't take the straight road — and I'm glad. My career has stretched across mortgage underwriting, restaurant operations, and insurance consulting. Each chapter felt unrelated at the time. Looking back, they were all the same lesson: understand the person, understand the risk, then make a decision you can defend.

Today at Allianz, I handle 50+ daily policy transactions, resolve complex account escalations, and build Power BI dashboards to surface insights from operational data - all while keeping everything compliant and audit-ready.

What's new is what's happening outside of work. I'm actively building a technical foundation in machine learning engineering and analytics — writing Python, modeling in SQL, building pipelines with dbt and Snowflake, and visualizing insights in Power BI. I'm not aspiring to be a data person. I'm becoming one.

What makes me different from a traditional data analyst: I bring real domain context from financial services. I've sat across from the person the data represents. That changes how you ask the question.
